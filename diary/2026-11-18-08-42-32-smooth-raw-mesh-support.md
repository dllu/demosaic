# Smooth RAW support and false cuts near intensity steps

Recorded: 2026-11-18T08:42:32-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

Audited the actual smooth mask nominated from Bayer samples. A flexible
background suppresses false support changes on the development cases.
On 24 new exact-target pairs, the distributed smooth mask reduces ISO400
added gap RMS from 81.21 to 39.40 DN16 and fades continuous wires near
steps. A shared step selected from reserved samples brings added gap
RMS to 32.66 in a retrospective replay, with all six continuous outputs
restored. The ISO6400 g162 gap fails the held-out audit even with its
true support and geometry supplied. The published masks remain
experimental. [Methods, exact-target triplets and scores](../docs/smooth-mesh-support.md).

Opened both sets of eight standing GFX views. The right cab retains
both directions with uneven reductions in contrast. The column step
replay keeps both left crops pixel-identical to the adjacent crop helper.
The main sky, building and foliage keep their earlier pixels. No new
checkpoint was trained for this study.
