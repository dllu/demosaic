# Pool Bayer support across neighboring Fourier bins

Recorded: 2026-11-19T21:44:50-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

Fit and verify complex cross-power across neighboring Fourier bins
on alternating exterior RAW windows. The joint gate uses that pooled
evidence and keeps each delivery bin's own measured phase.

On 32 unused backgrounds at the frozen flat-error budget, joint
gating gains 1.004 weak-response points and reduces error on the
continuing direction by 2.979 DN16 versus the earlier four-grid
mean. False weak projection changes by +0.011 DN16. Dense cosine
keeps more intact response. Natural-photo fine and flat errors rise
with joint gating. Both standing scenes were viewed at native size.
The sky remains smooth and the real cab still lacks fine crossings.
