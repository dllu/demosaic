# Dense nearby Bayer donors

Kept the 32-pixel filter and the Corpus 100k EMA fixed. Reserving
a dense nearby lattice increases the nominal left cab change from
0.057 to 0.287 DN16 RGB RMS and strengthens signed response at both
observed frequencies. The improvement stays small at equal correction
RMS. The crossings remain incomplete.

At the tight donor budget, denser sampling lowers continuing-wire
error inside genuine gaps by 0.199 DN16 versus a uniform atlas.
Separately, stronger proximity on that uniform atlas adds 0.242
short-wire response points. Donor-only confirmation costs response at
a matched flat budget, and the natural-photo transfer retains higher
increment and flat RMS than the frozen parent.

Viewed all 12 standing native boards and both long and short exact-wire
comparisons. The prior uniform camera boards reproduce at display
precision. The CPU study adds no training or fixed-panel PSNR score.
The general recommendation stays V120.
