# 86k ADC lift and noise crossover

Recorded: 2026-11-18T01:14:33-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

The four-material ADC review favored the 86k checkpoint. A predeclared
64-photo subset of the existing faint simulation tested that gain with
all four Bayer orders and unchanged batch shapes. Raw 86k gains 0.518 dB
in mean sRGB PSNR and raises weakest-band error from 2.868 to 3.027 DN16
against V120. Bin Malvar takes 86k to 2.208 DN16, versus 2.269 for V120
with the same physical projection. The 86k projection improves all 64
photo PSNRs.

Bin bilinear and bin sites preserve the exact same measured values as
the established bin Malvar mode. The site-only arm has higher PSNR on
the original four-material spot panel, while bin Malvar gives both the
highest mean PSNR and lowest weakest-band error on the broader subset.
The original 288 spot metrics and 1,024 V120 photo metrics match exactly.
The first spot replay rendered the wrong number of panel labels, and
the first photo reduction used an incorrect metric key. The reruns
preserve the same calculations and input predictions. Both failed-stage
sources and logs are retained.

Across the 147-color, four-CFA grid, bright yellow RGGB reaches
1,429.92 DN16 raw bias despite the earlier two-color recovery. Bin
Malvar lowers the full-grid maximum to 12.87 DN16. Bilinear lowers
centered RMS further. The optional flat-aware mode matches all 1,176
bilinear outputs on the palette exactly and stays within 0.0001 dB
of bin Malvar on the photos. Its spatial test selects 2.22% of the
packed photo sites. The focused software suite passes 101 tests.

The 48 matched positive-noise inputs retain the original faint targets.
86k lowers fine-band RMS at the smallest noise prior. The ISO100 and
ISO6400 priors give slightly higher fine-band error. The ISO100 bark
ridges and fur strands are soft in both native methods. The first noise
preparation read a missing name on unrelated rows. The final selector
uses the declared ADC-only rows and retains the same seeded recipe.
All four bin policies bypass the positive-noise sweep and both standing
GFX sets exactly. The 100k training run has passed 90,000 updates.
