# Use nearby RAW phase when distant phase fails

Recorded: 2026-11-20T05:46:21-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

The [nearby phase study](../docs/local-near-phase.md) keeps the entire
256-pixel noisy recipient fixed while changing distant phase. On 32 new
photo backgrounds with shifted distant phase, a direction-weighted collar
raises faint contrast recovery by 0.719 points and lowers intact weak RMS
by 0.568 DN16. Added gap luma falls from 0.119 to 0.094 DN16, with a paired
interval spanning zero. At matched added gap luma, the recovery difference
is 1.143 points. At fixed frequencies, the nearby measurements reproduce
bit for bit under the distant swap.

The opt-in `weak-near-phase` crop fit matches the experimental output
within 0.002 DN16 in 776 crop and context combinations. The [main GFX
scene](../docs/gfx-qualitative.md) keeps its smooth sky and connected coarse
crossings. Both [right cab views](../docs/gfx-green-mesh.md) qualify for
nearby phase and redistribute weak diagonal contrast locally. Fine crossings
remain incomplete. The report includes two native exact-target triplets
and both standing reviews.

This crop study uses frozen Local 50k EMA weights. The broad panels in
the [PSNR history](../docs/performance-history.md) fall outside this
evaluation. [Frozen sources and receipts](../docs/results/local-near-phase-v1/publication.json).
