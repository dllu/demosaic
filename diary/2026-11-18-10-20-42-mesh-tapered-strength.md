# Limit refitted rear strength outside credible gaps

Recorded: 2026-11-18T10:20:42-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

The taper reduces outside-gap attenuation from 9.57 to 7.02 DN16
on 24 independent high-noise gap draws, with residual rear-support
error increasing from 42.49 to 43.75. The separate-strength fit
improves fresh ISO6400 gap error from 221.24 to 220.79 DN16 and
reduces exterior attenuation. At equal delivered strength, both
methods yield small exterior gains. The native g223 footprint plot
shows a cut that still misses the true gap angle. An index in the
initial geometry preflight was corrected before the panel plan
and measurements were recorded.

The taper keeps the nominal right-cab pixels and weakens the
uniform refit's small cuts in the other cab crops. All eight
standing camera crops and both exact synthetic triplets were
opened. The result is experimental and adds no new full-photo
benchmark score. The four fixed benchmark collectors are active.
