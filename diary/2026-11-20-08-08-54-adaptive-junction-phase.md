# Select crossing phase on independent Bayer samples

Recorded: 2026-11-20T08:08:54-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

## Results

A reserved Bayer fold selected the crossing phase with no fallback
after held rejection. On 24 new variable-phase photos, it added
0.335 contrast points and lowered junction error 0.203 DN16 over
regional RAW refit. The fixed curve added 0.371 points and peaked
at 0.328 DN16 of gap residue versus 0.145 for selection. The
equal-gap response interval included zero. Testing the difference
band locally gave less recovery than scaling the whole-region pool
to the same measured gap luma.

Both real right-cab profiles gave a near-tied vote for curvature.
A 0.5 predictive SD margin selected affine, with small local changes
and incomplete fine crossings. Both GFX families and the exact
triplets were viewed at native crop size. The pilot camera boards
were regenerated byte-for-byte after widening the published board;
the published transferred profiles include both JPEG references.
The odd-origin fold audit passed on all four Bayer layouts.
The focused carrier and observability suite passed all 79 tests.

This was a crop inference study of frozen Local 50k EMA and the
regional RAW refit. The [full-model performance history](../docs/performance-history.md)
continues to track the comparable full panels.
