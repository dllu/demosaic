# Fit phase geometry across opposing RAW donors

Recorded: 2026-11-17T18:11:15-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

Shared phase geometry interpolated from neighboring RAW improves
ISO400 far weak recovery from 9.38% to 12.13% against the paired
fixed planar fit. The weak-increment RMS falls from 11.189 to
11.172 DN16 at equal quiet error. The ISO6400 far increment
error rises slightly.

The first joint check accepted false curvature on four straight
donor pairs with different periods. Checking each donor against its
own held-out planar fit rejects all four and preserves all 160
prediction arrays on the retained main panel. The camera crops
mostly use the guarded fallback. The transferred-profile left cab
changes slightly with horizontal donors and retains incomplete fine
crossings. I viewed both bracket placements and their native
neighborhoods. [Opposing-donor report](../docs/bracket-phase-geometry.md).
