# Corpus 100k FP16 batch size at fixed tile geometry

Recorded: 2026-11-19T19:10:29-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

Replayed the Corpus 100k EMA on all 520 DSCF3879 tiles. TensorRT FP16
on the trunk and DINO cut the paired tile loop from 123.35 to 103.90 s
at batch four. Both batch-four exports match their earlier TIFFs by
hash. Eager batch eight ties batch four and increases peak PyTorch CUDA
allocation from 2.31 to 4.06 GB. Compiled batch two brings allocation
to 1.21 GB and takes 3.7% longer than compiled batch four.

Reviewed both standing GFX families, including both cab noise profiles,
both JPEG references and two display gains. Main sky and fence agree
visually across paths. Cab crossings remain incomplete. The largest
numeric differences cluster around clipped lighting. The numerical
review covers the full RGB16 export, six exact photo contexts and
24 controlled wire fields. The supported Corpus 100k setting is eager
FP16 at 512 pixels, 64 overlap and batch four. Both compiled engines
use the experimental driver.
