0.315 points to green-only at equal added gap luma across the
24 new variable-phase fixtures. It gains 0.195 points at equal
gap luma over the whole-region pool. At the same dose the
shared case gains 0.035 points with lower gap luma, while the
red-continuing case loses 0.072 points with higher gap luma.

the red sites and held the regional ancestor fixed. In one
continuing-red draw, coherent red restores a green taper,
reducing gap luma from 1.246 to 0.159 DN16. Green-only outputs
were identical in every pair. All four Bayer layouts passed
the held-information audit, and all 79 focused carrier and
observability tests passed.

Both GFX families and two native exact triplets were viewed.

assistance. Fine crossings remain incomplete. This was a
