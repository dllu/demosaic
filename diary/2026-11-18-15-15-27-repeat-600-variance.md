# Check repeat variance at matched response on separate photos

Recorded: 2026-11-18T15:15:27-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

Scored the 600-update repeat EMA using the original 64
photos to set the response scale and 32 additional sources to
test it. The 0.25 repeat weight lowers nominal independent
variance by 102.4 DN16² with lower aligned response. At matched
response on the additional photos, variance instead rises by
17.5 DN16² and increment RMS is nearly unchanged.

Of the starting head's 315.2 DN16² added variance on the
additional photos, twice the parent-correction covariance
contributes 234.3 DN16² and correction variance contributes
80.9 DN16². Both training runs continue. This result is an
interim numerical review.
