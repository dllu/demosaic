Recorded: 2026-11-17T21:59:04-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

Used only the quarter of query RAW already set aside for screening to
locate a finite footprint. The best candidate must improve held-out
reserve error against both a uniform fundamental fit and a no-carrier
fit. The other three-quarters independently determine wave admission.
Replayed the prior eight geometries and eight fresh offset geometries
at each of two noise levels, plus 48 deliberately misleading rail
and flat controls.

6.59 to 6.01 DN16 on the prior panel and 5.90 to 5.77 on the offset panel
under the uniform quiet limit. Strong-wire response stays essentially
unchanged and forced-guide rail RMS is close to uniform. The ISO6400
weak response does not transfer to the new offset cases.

Reviewed both native synthetic triplets and both GFX families. The sky
remains exact to V120. The lower-margin spectral footprint adds a faint
building modulation, which the confirmed variant avoids. Partner-only
delivery preserves the coarse lattice and adds no fine cab crossings.
Existing checkpoint weights were used and the full-model score history
does not change.
