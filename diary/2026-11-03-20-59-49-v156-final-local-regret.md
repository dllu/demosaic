# V156 final local-regret review

Recorded: 2026-11-03T20:59:49-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

V156 completed both 1,000-update heads and all four fixed raw/EMA endpoints.
Local RGB-RMS regret reduces mean mixed quiet-neighbor error 0.30–0.63% versus
the matched delivered-RGB MSE control. Every one of the 32 quiet inputs improves
at each endpoint. Mean added regression above V143 falls about 71–98%, while
absolute quiet RMS remains 0.008–0.224% above V143. This is improved allocation
of error; it does not remove the parent's broad colored mottle. Against V143,
29/32 raw and 20/32 EMA quiet cases still regress.

Mesh response stays 5.59–10.52% above V143, reaching only 2.33–2.43% of exact
procedural GT. It gives up 5.24–5.81% response versus MSE. Actual mesh-blank RMS
is 0.010–0.062% below V143 and lower than MSE. Versus the quieter V151 control,
response improves 1.76–3.07% and mean quiet RMS falls 0.03–0.18%; raw wins 21/32
quiet cases and EMA 28/32, with individual costs retained in the paired audit.
The V151 comparison also changes relative inputs and base spectral supervision,
so only the exact matched MSE comparison isolates the new loss.

Native512 noisy-flat full RMS improves 0.116–0.172% versus V143, less than
V154's standalone-flat gain. Coarse flat changes remain tiny or mixed. Natural
band response exceeds MSE but stays 0.20–0.66% below V143, with mixed full/band
errors and visibly soft fine texture. All 192 constant ADC outputs are bit-exact
to their parents, which is not equality to GT. ADC-photo regression is reduced
versus MSE but still loses V150's stronger gain. ADC-slope full error improves,
while block32 error worsens 0.278–0.337% versus V143 and is also worse than MSE.

The real-photo result remains unresolved. All 72 primary figures were actually
viewed at original detail in 18 unscaled sheets. Fine cab crossings remain
missing; small numerical quiet gains do not visibly clean the broad blobs at
40× contrast. Both standing GFX scenes, sky/building/foliage/coarse fence, both
fine-cab windows, both priors and SOOC JPEGs remain in the comparison. Independent
ISO100 references differ in aperture, shutter and motion; they are qualitative
references and were never scored as exact GT. Protected captures, JPEGs and
siblings remain excluded from training; originals stay read-only.

two mesh, two flat and two quiet crops per endpoint. Every cell is 160×160

records source/ROI, checkpoint hashes, physical conditioning and display exposure.
The new README examples show local-EMA quiet neighbors for both ISO priors;
