# Along-wire and across-wire RAW validation

Recorded: 2026-11-17T12:32:47-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

The oriented-support test keeps the corrected guides and fitted RAW
predictions and changes only the footprint of held-out predictive
support. The square is compared with equal continuous area, two-to-one
rectangles along and across the wire. The measured lattice areas differ
from the square by fewer than seven sites before tile clipping.

The tight-budget ISO400 far weak response falls from 17.54% for the
square to 5.40% along and 7.00% across, with losses on all eight paired
geometries. At the same numerical threshold 2, the rectangles have
closer response but higher quiet error. At ISO6400 far, the across arm
has similar quiet error and lowers weak-increment RMS from 22.4643 to
22.4354 DN16, with response falling from 2.29% to 1.77%. The ISO6400
wire-free edge control shows nearly identical error across windows.
The sky stays identical to V120 and cab crossings remain incomplete.

All twelve native boards were opened. Independent checks cover 1,728
reductions, 80 old camera cells, four triplet subsets, and the square
outputs reproduce the previous threshold-2 results in all 392 views.
The 100k run remains active past 24,000 updates. The scoped long 20k
review remains the latest evaluation of that trajectory, with broader
photo evaluations still collecting.
