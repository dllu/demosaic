# Checking spatial phase inside weak wire crossings

Recorded: 2026-11-20T07:57:13-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

Tested whether a difference-frequency phase built from two moving
fundamentals predicts held RAW after accounting for a fixed carrier
with variable amplitude. On 24 new warped fixtures the curved fit
adds 0.215 contrast points and lowers junction increment error
0.115 DN16. Eight new stationary-junction controls and 24 earlier
straight fixtures reproduce the regional refit. A supplied-geometry
diagnostic adds 1.191 points. Pooling amplitude increases recovery
and exceeds the gap budget on transfer.

The right cab supports the simpler affine phase. The fitted affine
fields differ by about 0.018 cycles RMS between the separate
approximately registered exposures after removing a constant offset.
The fixed-dose curve leaves both cab crops at the regional refit.
The native comparisons retain incomplete fine crossings and mottle.
