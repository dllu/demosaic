# Test multiple disjoint RAW line guides

Recorded: 2026-11-18T04:33:53-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

The initial 96 exact pairs and the 48 fresh pairs test line geometry from
the exterior Bayer surround while keeping local RAW fixed across guide
conditions. In the initial ISO400 crossing panel, both wires improve
from 13.14% and 12.11% of the exact signed increment to 100.53% and
35.24%. The bank finds the exterior directions even at ISO6400, where
the unchanged local fit recovers little. It also offers false offset
peaks and can add a correction where a strong step replaces a wire.

Checking both native RAW shoulders separately at each end preserves the
fresh ISO400 gains and removes the measured false step correction. Gaps
still have some residual extension. Both searches leave the sky and
all four cab crops identical to the local parent. The shoulder check
retains one small building correction. I viewed both versions of each
standing GFX crop and selected synthetic native triplets. The fine green
cab crossings remain incomplete.
