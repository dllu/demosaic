# Whole-frame paths for RAW donor ranking

Recorded: 2026-11-20T03:25:07-08:00 (America/Los_Angeles).

## Decision

Keep whole-frame endpoint appearance as the stronger donor-ranking reference.
The graph adds a scene-dependent ranking signal in the controlled occlusion
layout, but its weak ISO400 response on the original panel is 0.89% versus
4.80% for endpoint appearance under the same quiet allowance. The two graphs
give identical delivered arrays on all 16 synthetic and eight camera inputs.

The layout intervention changes both intended-donor selections from 6/8 with
the matching routes open to 2/8 after swapping the corridors. Query and donor
RAW stay fixed. The real left cab changes its selected pair only because the
restricted graph truncates interpolation at the neighborhood boundary. Padding
by one cell makes affinity, selected windows and output exact. Both graphs
retain the coarse fence and leave the fine crossings incomplete.

## Record

The existing V143 and V120 EMAs were evaluated with fixed shared donor and query
validation. Numerical review, 10 native comparison boards, two native triplets
and one layout preview are complete. Training and fixed photo or camera PSNR
panels were outside this inference-only comparison.
