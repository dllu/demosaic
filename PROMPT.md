# Joint demosaicing and denoising from free images

This project aims to implement joint demosaicing and denoising from free images.
The goals:

- State of the art quality for demosaicing
- Supports 16 bit
- Needs to be qualitatively way, way, way, way better than RawNIND which sucks and was trained on very few images
- Fast speed (100 megapixels within 10 seconds on this NVIDIA GB10) (however, quality is more important, as long as 100 megapixel 16 bit photos are possible to demosaic and denoise properly)

The main assumptions:

- Most modern digital camera noise is largely Poissonian. Shot noise by far dominates other noise sources like thermal noise for typical photos.

Data strategy:

- We have a corpus of freely licensed images from Wikimedia Commons Quality Images at /mnt/external/qi.
This is an incomplete download generated by `~/proj/pupphoto/download_quality_images.py`. You can resume the download_quality_images script should you need additional data.
- We should aggressively bin these images by a factor of, say, 8x8 in order to gain additional bit depth and sharpness
- The joint demosaicing and denoising algorithm can operate on small tiles of, say, 256 x 256 or similar, and be blended afterwards for high resolution photos
- We can augment our data with:
  - Synthetic data, like textures, shapes, fine lines, and so on
  - Data augmentation of the images, including:
    - Rotations, non-aspect-ratio-preserving scaling, and homographies as long as they do not stretch the image excessively in a way that undoes the benefits of the earlier binning step
    - Motion blur
    - Radial blur
    - Kernel blur with simulated optical coma, astigmatism, and other effects that introduce blurring but still retain high frequency detail
    - Gaussian blur
    - Exposure adjustments leading to overexposure
      - Our model must be able to reconstruct the boundaries around overexposed areas without false color fringing.
    - Exposure adjustments leading to underexposure
- Apply a Bayer mask to each of these cleaned and augmented images
- Add simulated Poisson noise consistent with theoretical amounts for various ISO levels + pixel size

  - Other agents may be working on other projects on the same machine.
    - When profiling, be aware that sometimes other agents may be consuming CPU
