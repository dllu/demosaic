# Pooled green-phase frequency selection (V187)

The completed [V186 diagnostic](frequency-selection-coverage.md) found that widening the frequency band alone helped little. V187 instead pools the two green phases and nine neighboring FFT powers before selecting the wave readout's sparse basis. **The matched trial is complete:** [actual quality](pooled-phase-wave-comparison.md) improves mesh response 5.23% raw / 3.64% EMA over V185 and reduces EMA quiet spatial RMS 0.48–0.52%. Absent/flat errors rise slightly; missing real crossings, false modulation and broad mottle remain. This is the current experimental wave architecture, with V185 and earlier quieter strengths retained. [Preparation receipts](results/v187-pooled-wave-preparation-v1.json).

Each phase's Hann/affine Fourier power uses the supplied noise variance. Their equal mean is smoothed over a circular 3×3 frequency neighborhood. Eight unique selected centers retain the native 1/16–1/3 cycles/pixel band and conjugate 3×3 suppression. Alternating ranks populate the two phase-order groups. Pooling, smoothing, unique allocation and rank ordering change together. Both green phases participate in selection; neither is a holdout, and these selected powers are not calibrated confidence. Neighboring powers can cross the center eligibility boundary.

Only the new wave basis and its support measurements use those bins. The frozen V183 matching baseline retains its original one-phase evidence exactly. V187 keeps V185's 508-parameter, 18→16→12 readout, large 65-packed-pixel wave window and smaller 33/17-packed-pixel contexts on an eight-packed-pixel grid. All 189 inherited tensors remain frozen/eval, with identical fresh head initialization and 193 state tensors. No target or reference enters inference.

The trial started afresh from V183 EMA, using the same 1,000 cached rows, 900 noisy updates, 3,600 images, 100 ADC skips, nine losses, masks, domain weights, AdamW schedule and head EMA clock as V185. Primary comparisons match raw/raw and EMA/EMA to V185. The completed 822-case review retains all earlier strengths and tests absent mesh, quiet fields, standalone flats, photographic detail and ADC controls. These real references are qualitative, not exact GT.

Two green phases cannot distinguish all four aliases, but this does not establish aliases as the cause of weak crossings. Prioritize separate evidence for local measurements and actual readout/objective behavior. No model or quality result changes.
